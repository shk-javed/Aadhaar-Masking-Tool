🔍 How It Works

1️ Upload Aadhaar Image

You simply upload a photo or scan of your Aadhaar card. Supported formats: JPG, PNG, PDF (single-page).

2️ OCR Text Extraction

The system uses PaddleOCR (a deep learning–based OCR engine) to extract text from the uploaded file. It is more accurate than Tesseract and works even for tilted, blurry, or vernacular Aadhaar card images.

3️ Masking the First 8 Digits

Our masking engine finds and covers the first 8 digits of the Aadhaar number with a white or blurred mask, leaving only the last 4 digits visible.

4️Download Secure Copy

You can preview and download the masked image for secure use or document submission. No data is stored on the server.
